---
title: "The Lighthouse Keeper's Guide to On-Call"
description: A lighthouse keeper's habits for the person holding the pager. Watch the lamp, log the weather and call for help early.
tags: [on-call, operations]
---

A keeper does not wait for the storm. The keeper trims the lamp every night, writes down what the sea did and signals the shore at the first sign of trouble.

On-call works the same way. Small, steady habits beat heroic nights.
